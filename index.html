Mediastorm B.V., Keurenplein 41, 1069 CD Amsterdam, The Netherlands, is responsible for your data.

What we hold: your name, business email address, business phone number, company name and public details about your business.

Where it comes from: public business listings, such as Google Maps, and your company's own website.

Why we use it: to contact you about our service for your business. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a relevant service to businesses in your trade.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to being contacted by email, phone or any other channel, by writing to mark@trymediastorm.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
